About this template

Made by a working hospitality shift leader. This kit gives a small café, restaurant, food truck or kitchen manager one simple spreadsheet for the numbers that decide whether a week was profitable.

Enter ingredients the way you actually buy them (a 2 L bottle, a 5 kg box, a tray of 30). The sheet works out cost per gram, millilitre or piece after yield, so your plate costs include trim and cooking loss.

Build each menu item from those ingredients and see the plate cost, a suggested price for your target food cost %, your real food cost % at your current price, and profit per plate. A simple menu-engineering view sorts items into Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles and Dogs so you know what to feature, fix or cut.

Every week, enter sales, stock and labour to see cost of goods %, labour % and prime cost %, with red flags when you go over your targets. A waste log (also as a printable PDF for the kitchen wall) shows where food is being thrown out and why.

Questions

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Microsoft Excel (desktop or online) and in Google Sheets, which is free. The Start Here tab shows how to open it in Google Sheets.

Does it work outside Australia?

Yes. Set your sales tax rate in Settings (10% GST, 20% VAT, your local sales tax, or 0% if menu prices exclude tax). The $ sign is just a number format; the maths works in any currency.

Is this connected to my POS?

No. You type or paste in your weekly totals. That keeps it simple and works with any POS or rostering system.

How many menu items can it hold?

Up to 100 menu items, 200 ingredients and 300 recipe lines out of the box. You can copy rows down to add more.

Is the menu engineering the full method?

It's a simplified version that compares each item's sales and profit with your menu average. It's a quick, practical guide rather than a consultant's report.

Can I get a refund?

Because it's an instant digital download, there are no change-of-mind refunds. If the file is faulty or not as described, contact us and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you.
