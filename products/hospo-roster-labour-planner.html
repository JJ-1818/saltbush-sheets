About this template

Labour is the other half of prime cost, but most rosters are built without knowing what they'll cost until the pay run. This planner puts the cost next to every shift while you're still building the week.

Add your team once with their weekday, Saturday, Sunday and public holiday rates (from your award or agreement). Then roster each shift with a start time, finish time and unpaid break. The sheet works out paid hours, picks the right day rate, and adds wages, super and an on-cost estimate for each shift.

Enter forecast sales for each day and the Dashboard shows labour cost as a % of forecast, day by day, against your own target. Add actual sales afterwards to see how the week really went. There's also a breakdown by role and each staff member's hours for the week, flagged if they're over their max hours.

It's a planning tool, not payroll software: you enter the rates, and it doesn't calculate overtime, allowances or junior rates. Example data is included so you can see how it works before you start. Designed to pair with the Saltbush Sheets food cost kit so you can watch both halves of prime cost.

Questions

Does it include award or penalty rates?

No. You enter each person's hourly rates for weekdays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. Check your award with the Fair Work Ombudsman. The example rates are made up for demonstration.

Is it payroll software?

No. It's for planning and costing the roster. It doesn't do overtime, allowances, junior rates, minimum engagement or pay slips. Shifts under 3 or over 10 hours are highlighted so you can check them against your award.

How do I enter shift times?

As decimal hours in 24-hour time: 6.5 is 6:30am, 14.75 is 2:45pm. Shifts that finish after midnight are handled automatically.

Does it work outside Australia?

Yes. Set super to 0% (or your local equivalent) and enter your own rates and on-costs.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open it in the free Google Sheets app. Building a full roster is easier on a laptop.

Does it go with the food cost kit?

Yes, it's designed to sit alongside the Saltbush Sheets food cost kit so you can track both labour and food cost. Each file works on its own.
