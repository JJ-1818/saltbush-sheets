About Saltbush Sheets

Saltbush Sheets is a small, independent template studio based in Australia. We make practical spreadsheet templates for small businesses and everyday money: the kind of tool you wish someone had handed you on day one.

Our hospitality templates draw on real, hands-on experience of how cafés and quick-service restaurants run day to day.

Every template is built to be honest and simple: working formulas, clear instructions, example data, and no promises we can't keep.
