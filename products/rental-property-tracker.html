About this template

For everyday landlords with one to ten properties who want clean records without paying for property software.

Log rent as it arrives and expenses as they're paid. Each row is sorted into your tax year automatically: set the start month to 7 for Australia's 1 July year, or 1 for a calendar year.

The Summary tab shows rent received, ongoing expenses, net cash flow, capital spend, gross yield and net yield for each property, plus a total for every expense category to hand to your accountant. Capital improvements are kept separate from ongoing costs because they're usually treated differently for tax.

A maintenance log keeps every repair request from reported to done, with open urgent jobs highlighted, and lease-end dates turn amber 60 days out.
