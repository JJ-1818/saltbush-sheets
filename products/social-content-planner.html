About this template

For small business owners and solo creators who post 'when there's time' and want a simple system instead.

Set up to five content pillars (the themes you post about) with a target share for each. Plan every post in one calendar: platform, pillar, format, hook, caption, hashtags and call to action, then move it from Idea to Posted.

A few days after posting, add likes, comments, shares, saves and reach. The engagement rate calculates itself and the dashboard shows posts by status and platform, your pillar balance vs target, and your best-performing post.

Stuck for ideas? The Ideas Bank gives you somewhere to park them, and 60 ready-made post prompts for small businesses get you started. A printable PDF planner is included too.

Yes. Upload it to Google Sheets and share it.
