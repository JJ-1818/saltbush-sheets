About this template

Most budget templates assume the same pay every month. If you work casual shifts, gigs, deliveries or freelance jobs, that never happens, and a normal budget falls apart in the first slow week.

This planner works the other way round. Log every payment the day it lands and it automatically puts aside a % for tax (if none was taken out) and a % for savings, so you can see what's genuinely yours to spend.

The Monthly Plan shows what came in, what went out, what was left over and a running buffer balance, measured in 'months of essentials covered'. It also shows your lowest recent month, which is a safe amount to plan around.

Simple enough to use from your phone in Google Sheets, with example data so you can see how it works before you start.
