Refunds

Our templates are digital files you can download straight away, so we don't offer refunds if you simply change your mind.

This doesn't limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If a template is faulty, doesn't work as described, or isn't what the listing said, email [your business email] within a reasonable time with your order details. We'll fix it, send a replacement or give you a refund.

If you bought through a store platform (such as Gumroad), the refund will be processed through that platform.
