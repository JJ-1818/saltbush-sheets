Questions

How does the calculator work it out?

Litres a week = km a week × L/100km ÷ 100. Fuel a week = litres × price. Yearly cost = fuel a week × 52 + rego + insurance + servicing. Per week = yearly cost ÷ 52. Per km = yearly cost ÷ (km a week × 52).

Is this advice?

No. It's a general estimate from the numbers you enter. Your real costs depend on your car, where you drive and what you pay.

What do I need to run the Car & Fuel Tracker?

A web browser on a computer: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It's one file that works offline, and your entries are saved in that browser.
