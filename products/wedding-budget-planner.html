About this template

Wedding costs creep up one booking at a time. This planner keeps the whole picture in one place so you always know what's booked, what's paid and what's still owing.

Start with estimates, then add the real price as you book. Over-budget items turn red, and any balance due in the next 30 days turns amber so nothing sneaks up on you. A contingency % keeps a buffer for surprises.

The guest list tracks invitations by household, RSVPs, meal choices, dietary notes, tables, gifts and thank-you cards. The dashboard totals attending guests and meals for your caterer.

A starter checklist from 12+ months out to after the day is included and fully editable, with printable PDFs of the checklist and guest list if you like pen and paper.
